Grocery Bot
The Grocery Bot is available now as a warm-up. Build a bot that controls a swarm of workers in a grocery store — pick up items, deliver orders, maximize your score.
How it works:

Go to the Challenge page, pick a difficulty, click Play
Copy the WebSocket URL and connect your bot
Your bot receives game state each round and responds with actions
Best score per map is saved — leaderboard = sum of all best scores

Read the full Grocery Bot documentation for the complete API spec, game mechanics, and example code.
